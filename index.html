FirstIn

Be first to apply at the companies you actually want.

FirstIn watches company job boards directly and sends you a notification within minutes of a new matching role — before the crowd finds it.

Coming soon to the Chrome Web Store

How it works

  • Pick companies: one-click popular companies, a “Watch” button on careers pages, or paste a Greenhouse, Lever or Ashby link.
  • Set filters: job title keywords, words to exclude, locations and remote.
  • Get notified when a matching job is posted, then apply in one click.

Private by design

No account, no tracking. Your companies, filters and saved jobs stay in your browser.
