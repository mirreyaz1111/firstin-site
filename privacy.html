FirstIn Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Short version: FirstIn does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any of your data. Everything you enter stays in your own browser.

What FirstIn stores

To work, FirstIn saves the following using Chrome's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device only:

  • The companies you choose to watch
  • Your filters (keywords, excluded words, locations, remote preference, check interval)
  • The jobs found for you, and whether you marked them as applied or hidden

This data never leaves your browser. Removing the extension deletes it.

Network requests

FirstIn only contacts the public job board APIs of the companies you watch:

  • boards-api.greenhouse.io
  • api.lever.co
  • api.ashbyhq.com

These requests only ask for a company's list of open jobs. They contain no personal information about you. Like any website request, those services can see your IP address; their own privacy policies apply to that.

Pages you visit

When you open the FirstIn popup on a Greenhouse, Lever or Ashby careers page, FirstIn reads that tab's address to offer a one-click "Watch" button. It does not read, record or change the content of any web page, and it does not track your browsing.

What FirstIn does not do

  • No accounts or sign-up
  • No analytics, tracking, cookies or advertising
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone
  • No remote code: everything that runs is included in the extension package

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. If a future version adds optional features that need a server (for example email alerts), they will be opt-in and described here before release.
